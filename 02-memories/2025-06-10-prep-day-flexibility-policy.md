# Prep Day Flexibility — Billing Policy Statement

**Source:** 2025-06-10-Prep_Day_Flexibility.md

## Summary

Short thread where Christian dictated a position statement on prep day billing policy, which was reformatted into a clean policy statement.

## Policy Statement (Refined)

Prep days — whether a month in advance or the day before production — must remain **billed hourly** because their scope is unpredictable. While prep days generally stay within targeted hour ranges, exceptions occur when other crew members leave early and Christian stays to complete the job.

**Core principle:**
> Prep days should have a targeted set of hours, but allow for extended billing when I stay to ensure the job gets done — I should not absorb cost that results from others leaving early.

## Key Points

- Prep day billing: **hourly** (not flat-rate) due to unpredictability
- Typical prep days stay within margins — but not always
- When crew dips out: Christian stays → billable time must be honored
- Position: total willingness to work however long it takes — but not at personal financial cost
