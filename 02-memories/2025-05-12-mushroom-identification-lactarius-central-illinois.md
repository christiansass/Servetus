# Mushroom Identification — Lactarius Foraging in Quincy IL

**Source:** 2025-05-12-Mushroom_Identification_Help.md
**Date:** 2025-05-12

## Summary

User submitted photos of a mushroom found foraging in central Illinois (Quincy area) for identification.

## Visual Characteristics

- **Cap:** Orange/reddish with reticulated (cracked, veiny) pattern
- **Gills:** Salmon/pinkish, close, free from stem
- **Stem:** Short, stubby, slightly off-center
- **Size:** ~2.5–3 inches wide (verified by ruler in photo)

## Possible Identification

**Most likely:** *Lactarius deliciosus* (Saffron Milk Cap) or *Lactarius indigo*

Key traits:
- Orange/pinkish gills match Lactarius genus
- Cracked/reddish cap consistent
- Central Illinois is within native range (mixed hardwood/pine areas)
- Lactarius species exude colored milk (latex) when cut — orange in *L. deliciosus*, blue in *L. indigo*

## Safety Warning

**Do not consume without expert confirmation.** Toxic lookalikes exist in *Lactarius* and *Russula* families with similar colorations and gill structures.
