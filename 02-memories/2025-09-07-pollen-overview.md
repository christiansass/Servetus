# Pollen — General Overview

**Source:** 2025-09-07-Pollen_information_summary.md
**Date:** 2025-09-07

## Summary

Brief general information session on pollen — what it is, types, ecological role, and human impact.

## What Pollen Is

- Fine powder produced in male plant structures (anthers)
- Carries male gametes for fertilization; enables seed/fruit production
- Tough protective wall helps grains survive until reaching female counterparts

## Types

- **Anemophilous (wind-pollinated)**: Light, dry; e.g., grasses, birch, ragweed — primary allergy culprits
- **Entomophilous (insect-pollinated)**: Heavy, sticky; e.g., roses, sunflowers — clings to pollinators

## Human Impact

- **Allergies**: Wind-pollinated pollen triggers hay fever (sneezing, itchy eyes, congestion)
- **Agriculture**: Crop yields depend on pollination
- **Industry/Medicine**: Used in supplements, skincare, forensic botany

## Fun Fact

Pollen fossils can survive millions of years; used to reconstruct past climates.
