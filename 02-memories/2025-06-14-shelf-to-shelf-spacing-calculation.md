# Shelf-to-Shelf Spacing Calculation — Stairway Shelves

**Source:** 2025-06-14-Shelf-to-shelf_spacing_calculation.md
**Date:** 2025-06-14

## Summary

Calculating equal spacing for two shelves hung on a wall perpendicular to a staircase.

## Given

- **Total vertical space:** 7′ 2 11/16″ (measured along a blue line perpendicular to stairs)
- **Number of shelves:** 2
- **Shelf thickness:** ½″ each → 1″ total

## Calculation

1. Convert total space: (7 × 12) + 2 + 11/16 = **86 11/16 inches**
2. Subtract shelf thickness: 86 11/16″ − 1″ = **85 11/16 inches**
3. Divide into 3 equal segments (floor → shelf 1 → shelf 2 → top): 85 11/16 ÷ 3 = **≈28 11/16″**

## Result

→ **Each shelf-to-shelf space: 28 11/16″** (28 17/24″ precise)

## Installation Notes

- Start from bottom reference point
- Measure up 28 11/16″ for bottom of first shelf
- Measure another 28 11/16″ for bottom of second shelf
- Account for ½″ shelf thickness when anchoring brackets
