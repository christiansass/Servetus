# Internet Service Ownership Analysis — GoDaddy, Network Solutions, InterNIC

**Source:** 2025-06-10-Internet_Service_Ownership_Analysis.md
**Date:** 2025-06-10
**Project:** Binary Ranch

## Summary

Christian challenged: "When was the last time anyone looked into core internet service ownership as deeply as they've scrutinized government?" Research into money interests and leadership allegiances of GoDaddy, Network Solutions, and InterNIC/ICANN.

## GoDaddy (NYSE: GDDY)

- **Ownership:** ~95.71% institutional investors; ~0.47% insiders; ~3.82% public
- **CEO:** Aman Bhutani (joined 2019; previous roles at Expedia, JPMorgan Chase)
- **Board:** Bhutani also serves on The New York Times Company board (audit/finance committees)

## Network Solutions

- **Ownership:** Subsidiary of Web.com → Newfold Digital (private equity-backed)
- **Leadership:** Limited public info; previously Gabriel Battista (CEO), Bob Tadevich (President)
- **Transparency:** Private equity structure limits public accountability

## InterNIC / ICANN

- **Governance:** InterNIC is a registered service mark of the U.S. Department of Commerce
- **Operated by:** ICANN (nonprofit, multistakeholder model)
- **Structure:** Global stakeholder involvement; less transparent than public companies

## Key Takeaway

GoDaddy: most transparent (public filings). Network Solutions: private equity — least accessible. ICANN: nonprofit but DoC-backed — government-adjacent.
