# Entropy — Explanation Across Physics, Information Theory, Cosmology, and Philosophy

**Source:** 2025-09-07-Entropy_explanation_types.md
**Date:** 2025-09-07

## Summary

Multi-domain explanation of entropy — asked as a single-word prompt. Offered scientific and spiritual/metaphorical dimensions, with invitation to explore the biblical worldview connection.

## Domains

### 1. Physics (Thermodynamics)
- Measure of disorder/randomness in a system
- **Second Law**: Total entropy of an isolated system always increases
- Natural processes (heat flow, ice melting) are irreversible — always move toward higher entropy

### 2. Information Theory
- Introduced by **Claude Shannon (1948)**
- Entropy = measure of uncertainty/unpredictability in information
- High entropy = random (unpredictable); Low entropy = ordered (predictable)
- Foundation of data compression, cryptography, communications

### 3. Cosmology
- **Heat death of the universe**: eventual maximum entropy — all energy evenly distributed, no useful work can be done, everything ceases to change

### 4. Philosophy / Spiritual Metaphor
- Entropy as symbol of **decay, corruption, disorder**
- Life, order, creation = **forces that push against entropy**
- Spiritually: sin, death, and the fall as entropic forces; redemption and resurrection as anti-entropic
