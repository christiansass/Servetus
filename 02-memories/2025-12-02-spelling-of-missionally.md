# Spelling of Missionally — Reference

**Source:** 2025-12-02-Spelling_of_missionally.md
**Date:** 2025-12-02

## Answer

**Missionally** — adverb from *missional* (common in church/organizational contexts)

Meaning: "in a way that is shaped by or aligned with a mission"

## Usage Examples

- "We want to live **missionally**, not just attend services."
- "The team is working **missionally** across departments."
- "She approaches filmmaking **missionally**, aiming to serve a clear purpose."

## Close Alternatives

- with a shared mission
- purposefully / with purpose
- mission-driven
