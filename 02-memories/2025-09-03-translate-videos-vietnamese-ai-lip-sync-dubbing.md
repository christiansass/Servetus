# Translate English Videos to Vietnamese — AI Lip-Sync Dubbing Workflow

**Source:** 2025-09-03-Translate_videos_with_dubbing.md
**Date:** 2025-09-03

## Summary

Workflow guide for translating English video content to Vietnamese with AI lip-sync dubbing (synchronized mouth movements).

## Workflow Steps

### Step 1: Transcription & Translation
- **Whisper (OpenAI)** — free, accurate English transcription
- **DeepL / Google Translate / ChatGPT** — translation to Vietnamese
- **HeyGen** — integrated voice dubbing with translation
- Note: Check religious or idiomatic expressions for accuracy (especially for biblical content)

### Step 2: Vietnamese Voiceover (TTS)
- **ElevenLabs** — natural Vietnamese voices; customizable emotion and pacing
- **PlayHT** — realistic AI voices with SSML control
- **Descript Overdub** — reusable voice clones and overdubs

### Step 3: Lip-sync Dubbing / Face Reanimation
- Tools listed in source file
- Goal: match speaker's mouth movements to the Vietnamese dub

## Key Consideration

Content with biblical themes requires careful idiomatic translation — literal word-for-word may lose meaning in Vietnamese.
