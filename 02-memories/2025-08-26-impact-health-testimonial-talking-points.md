# Impact Health — Customer Testimonial Talking Points (Roxanne Shilko)

**Source:** 2025-08-26-Testimonial_talking_points.md
**Date:** 2025-08-26

## Summary

Planning session with client Roxanne Shilko for a customer testimonial video for Impact Health — a health-share co-op (not traditional insurance). Bullet-point talking points for an iPad teleprompter/guide during the interview.

## What Is Impact Health?

- Health-share co-op model (not insurance)
- Works more like insurance in practice: card, payer ID, Impact handles negotiations and admin
- No shareholders or outside ROI demands
- CEO salary ~$250K vs. industry CEOs at $10M+ (legitimacy signal)

## Talking Points

### Introduction / Background
- Small, local "mom & pop" business owner (patriot)
- Previous traditional insurance costs

### Decision to Join
- Initial hesitation about legitimacy
- Key distinction: it's a *share*, not insurance
- No reimbursement model; no heavy admin burden on customer
- Impact handles negotiations directly

### Customer Experience
- Positive onboarding: HIPAA-trained rep, referral system, clear answers
- First real test: ER visit after husband's hand injury
  - Hospital staff confused by card ("What is this?")
  - Customer had to be firm: "Put in the payer ID — this is legitimate"
  - Impact negotiated charges; prevented balance billing
  - Impact stood up for customer in physical therapist billing dispute

### Financial Impact
- Previous premiums: ~$1,700/month
- Now: ~$600–700/month
- **Saving ~$1,000 every month**
- Especially compelling for healthy 50-something couple with few claims
