# macOS Menu Bar Management Utilities — Apple Silicon Guide

**Source:** 2025-09-13-Menu_bar_utilities_Apple_Silicon.md
**Date:** 2025-09-13

## Summary

Query about menu bar collapse/hide utilities for Apple Silicon Macs. Several options identified with different levels of control and price points.

## Options Compared

| App | Cost | Method | Notes |
|-----|------|--------|-------|
| **Hidden Bar** | Free | Threshold chevron toggle | Available Mac App Store or GitHub |
| **Bartender** | Paid | Secondary bar; full icon management | Most powerful/feature-rich |
| **Vanilla** | Free/Paid | Click to expand/hide threshold | Simpler than Bartender |
| **Dozer** | Free (open-source) | Dots as toggle markers | Open-source alternative |
| **iBar** | Varies | Similar toggle utility | Less well-known |

## Recommendation Notes

- For lightweight, free solution: **Hidden Bar** or **Dozer**
- For full control: **Bartender**
- All confirmed compatible with Apple Silicon at time of session
