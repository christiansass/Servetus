# Venezuelan Exodus — Causes and Return Risk

**Source:** 2026-01-04-Venezuelan_Exodus_Reasons.md
**Date:** 2026-01-04

## Summary

Two-part Q&A on the Venezuelan displacement crisis: causes of mass emigration and risks for returnees.

## Scale

- Nearly **8 million Venezuelans** living outside the country by late 2025
- ~1 in 4 Venezuelans has left — one of the largest displacement crises in the world
- Primary destinations: Colombia, Peru, Ecuador, Brazil; also U.S. and Europe

## Causes of Exodus

1. **Severe economic collapse** — hyperinflation, oil dependency mismanagement, destroyed savings
2. **Food, medicine, and services shortages** — basic goods unavailable; healthcare failing
3. **Political instability and human rights abuses** — suppression of dissent, political arrests
4. **High crime and insecurity** — armed robbery, kidnapping, gang violence
5. **Lack of economic opportunity** — unemployment, inflation eroding livelihoods
6. **Humanitarian emergency** — compound of all above factors

## Return Risk

- Depends on individual circumstances and political profile
- Those who fled due to **political persecution, opposition activity, or activism** face real risk of arrest if returning
- Others may face fewer formal barriers but still encounter unsafe conditions
