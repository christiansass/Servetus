# PayPal Donation Fees — Nonprofit vs. Standard Breakdown

**Source:** 2025-09-04-PayPal_donation_fees.md
**Date:** 2025-09-04

## Summary

Fee breakdown for receiving a $1,000 donation via PayPal under different account types.

## Fee Scenarios for $1,000 Donation

| Account Type | Rate | Net Received |
|-------------|------|-------------|
| **Nonprofit (registered)** | 1.99% + $0.49 | **$979.61** |
| **Standard Business/Personal** | 2.89% + $0.49 | **$970.61** |
| **International** | Add ~1.5% cross-border fee | Lower |
| **PayPal Giving Fund** | ~2% flat (different terms) | Varies |

## Key Notes

- Nonprofit rate requires registered charity status with PayPal
- High-volume nonprofits may qualify for 2.2% + $0.30 rate
- PayPal Giving Fund involves delayed disbursement and may not pass donor info
- International transactions add currency conversion fees on top
