# Weather-Resistant Shotgun Microphones — Buyer Guide

**Source:** 2025-09-28-Water-resistant_shotgun_mics.md
**Date:** 2025-09-28

## Summary

Query and guide on moisture/weather-resistant shotgun microphones for outdoor and adverse-condition shoots. True waterproof shotgun mics don't exist — RF-condenser design is the key for moisture resistance.

## Key Distinction

- **Waterproof/submersible** = hydrophone (different product entirely)
- **Weather/moisture-resistant** = what you want for rain, humidity, fog, condensation on shoots

## Top Recommended Mics

| Mic | Key Feature | Level |
|-----|------------|-------|
| **Sennheiser MKH 416** | RF-condenser design; highly immune to humidity | Pro / pricey |
| **RØDE NTG-3** | RF-bias; almost completely resistant to moisture; rugged case | Broadcast-grade |
| **Deity S-Mic 2 / 2S** | Budget-friendly; marketed as weather-proof circuitry | Mid-range |

## Buying Tips

- Look for **"RF-biased / RF condenser"** in spec sheet — best humidity resistance
- **Waterproof ≠ weatherproof** — don't expect moisture-resistant mics to survive submersion
- Use proper **blimp/deadcat windscreen** even with moisture-resistant mics
- Use **IP-rated protective case** for rainy shoots
