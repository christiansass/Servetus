# Messianic Prophecies — Emmaus Road Reconstruction (Moses through Prophets)

**Source:** 2026-02-17-Messianic_Prophecies_in_Luke.md
**Date:** 2026-02-17

## Summary

The user requested an enumeration of every OT prophecy Messiah may have expounded on the road to Emmaus (Luke 24), "beginning with Moses and all the prophets," specifically focusing on why he had to be crucified. Luke does not record which passages were used — only that they covered the law of Moses, the prophets, and the psalms. The response is a best-fit reconstruction anchored in Torah atonement patterns, Prophets/Writings suffering-servant passages, and every explicit "fulfilled" citation in the four Gospels. A separate companion note covers the Simon Peter cross-reference.

## Key Points — OT Passages by Section

**Genesis:** Gen 3:15 (seed bruised), Gen 22 (Isaac/substitution), Gen 37–50 (Joseph/suffering→glory pattern)

**Exodus:** Ex 12 (Passover lamb, no bone broken), Ex 24:6–8 (covenant blood)

**Leviticus:** Lev 1–7 (substitutionary offerings), Lev 16 (Day of Atonement), Lev 17:11 (blood/atonement)

**Numbers:** Num 21:4–9 (bronze serpent lifted up), Num 9:12 (no bone broken)

**Deuteronomy:** Deut 18:15–19 (prophet like Moses), Deut 21:22–23 (hanged one is cursed — explains crucifixion as curse-bearing)

**Psalms:** Ps 2 (anointed opposed then installed), Ps 16 (not abandoned to grave), Ps 22 (righteous sufferer — mocking, garment-casting, fulfilled in crucifixion), Ps 34:20 (no bone broken), Ps 41:9 (betrayal by companion, fulfilled John 13:18), Ps 69 (thirst/vinegar, fulfilled John 19:28), Ps 118:22–26 (rejected stone→cornerstone)

**Isaiah:** Isa 50:6 (beating/shame), Isa 52:13–53:12 (Suffering Servant — densest single "why crucified" text; numbered with transgressors fulfilled Mark 15:28), Isa 61:1–2 (fulfilled Luke 4:21)

**Jeremiah:** Jer 31:31–34 (New Covenant — remission logic)

**Daniel:** Dan 7:13–14 (Son of Man receives glory), Dan 9:24–26 (Anointed cut off)

**Hosea/Jonah:** Hos 6:2 (third day), Jon 1–2 (three days sign pattern)

**Zechariah:** Zech 9:9 (king on donkey, fulfilled Matt 21:4), Zech 11:12–13 (30 pieces of silver, fulfilled Matt 27:9), Zech 12:10 (pierced one), Zech 13:7 (shepherd struck/sheep scattered)

## Every "Fulfilled" in the Four Gospels

**Matthew (16):** 1:22 (Isa 7:14), 2:15 (Hos 11:1), 2:17 (Jer 31:15), 2:23 (Nazarene), 4:14 (Isa 9:1–2), 8:17 (Isa 53:4), 12:17 (Isa 42:1–4), 13:14 (Isa 6:9–10), 13:35 (Ps 78:2), 21:4 (Zech 9:9), 27:9 (Zech 11:12–13), 27:35 (Ps 22:18), plus general fulfillment statements at 5:18, 24:34, 26:54, 26:56

**Mark (4):** 1:15 (time fulfilled), 13:4 (prophetic timetable), 14:49 (general), 15:28 (Isa 53:12 — numbered with transgressors)

**Luke (8):** 1:20 (Gabriel/John's birth), 2:43 (calendar), 4:21 (Isa 61:1–2), 21:22 (judgment on Jerusalem), 21:24 (times of Gentiles), 21:32 (end-times), 22:16 (Passover in kingdom), 24:44 (Messiah's own summary — law, prophets, psalms)

**John (11):** 12:38 (Isa 53:1), 13:18 (Ps 41:9), 15:25 (Ps 35:19/69:4), 19:24 (Ps 22:18), 19:28 (Ps 69:21 — "I thirst"), 19:36 (Ex 12:46/Num 9:12/Ps 34:20 — no bone broken), plus sayings-of-Jesus fulfillments at 17:12, 17:13, 18:9, 18:32, and non-prophecy usage at 3:29
