# Silverado Crackling Noise — Exhaust / Catalytic Converter Diagnosis

**Source:** 2026-02-16-Crackling_Noise_Silverado.md
**Date:** 2026-02-16

## Summary

Troubleshooting session for a crackling noise from the rear of a Silverado truck. The session covered three possible causes — normal heat-cycle metal expansion, loose heat shield, exhaust leak, or failing catalytic converter — and explained which scenarios affect fuel economy. The session ended with diagnostic questions unanswered (noise timing, Silverado year), so no definitive resolution was reached.

## Key Points

- **Normal crackling (post-shutdown):** Metal cooling/contracting — exhaust pipes, heat shields, muffler. Normal, no mileage impact.
- **Crackling while driving (RPM-dependent):** Could be:
  - Loose heat shield (cheap fix, no mileage impact)
  - Exhaust leak (slight mileage drop, louder, may smell)
  - Failing catalytic converter (rattling ceramic inside, P0420 code, mileage drop, sluggish acceleration)
- **Bad cat → mileage:** Yes, if partially clogged/restricted; backpressure increases, engine works harder
- **DIY diagnostics:**
  - Cold tap test with rubber mallet — if loose material rattles inside, cat is failing
  - Scan for P0420 code even without check engine light
  - Track sudden mileage drops (2–5 mpg)
