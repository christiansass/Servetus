# Military Challenge Coin Databases — Research

**Source:** 2026-02-23-Military_Challenge_Coin_Databases.md
**Date:** 2026-02-23

## Summary

Brief research session on whether centralized databases exist for military challenge coins. No official government database exists — coins are issued at unit/command level. Several community-driven and commercial catalog resources were identified.

## Key Points

- **No official central database:** Challenge coins are issued locally (unit commanders, deployments, detachments) and not centrally tracked by any military branch
- **Best dedicated catalog:** ChallengeCoinED.com — searchable catalog with images, descriptions, market values; filterable by category and branch
- **General collectibles:** Colnect — collector-driven catalog; challenge coin coverage depends on contributor activity
- **Reference/ID sites:** OldCoinOnline, U.S. Military Challenge Coins Guide — branch and mission type listings
- **De facto database:** eBay — hundreds of military challenge coins browsable by keyword; useful for identification and valuation
- **Retail catalogs:** Challenge Coin Company, MilitaryBest.com, PriorService.com — branch-organized listings
- **Coverage caveat:** Community contribution model means many rare/unit-specific coins are not documented anywhere online
