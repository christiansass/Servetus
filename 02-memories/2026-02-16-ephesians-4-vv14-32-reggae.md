## Notes

Scripture-faithful KJV reggae arrangement. One-drop feel, ~74 BPM, D minor or E minor. Spacious feel, lines allowed to breathe. Stage directions in brackets.

## Lyrics

```
[Tempo: One-drop reggae, ~74 BPM]
[Key: D minor or E minor]
[Feel: Spacious, steady bass, allow lines to breathe]

[Verse 1 – v.14]
That we henceforth be no more children,
[hold]
Tossed to and fro,
[rest 1 bar]
And carried about with every wind of doctrine,
[hold]
By the sleight of men,
And cunning craftiness,
Whereby they lie in wait to deceive.
[2 bar instrumental]

[Chorus – vv.15–16a]
But speaking the truth in love,
[hold]
May grow up into Him in all things,
Which is the head,
Even Christ;
[rest]
From whom the whole body,
Fitly joined together,
[build slightly]

[Verse 2 – vv.16b–19]
And compacted by that which every joint supplieth,
[hold]
According to the effectual working
In the measure of every part,
[rest]
Maketh increase of the body
Unto the edifying of itself in love.
[2 bar instrumental]

This I say therefore,
And testify in the Lord,
[drop instruments slightly]
That ye henceforth walk not
As other Gentiles walk,
In the vanity of their mind,
[hold]

Having the understanding darkened,
Being alienated from the life of God,
Through the ignorance that is in them,
Because of the blindness of their heart:
[rest 1 bar]
Who being past feeling
Have given themselves over unto lasciviousness,
To work all uncleanness with greediness.
[2 bar instrumental]

[Chorus – fuller]
But speaking the truth in love,
May grow up into Him in all things,
Which is the head,
Even Christ;
From whom the whole body,
Fitly joined together,
Maketh increase of the body
Unto the edifying of itself in love.
[hold]

[Verse 3 – vv.20–24]
But ye have not so learned Christ;
[rest]

If so be that ye have heard Him,
And have been taught by Him,
As the truth is in Jesus:
[hold]

That ye put off
Concerning the former conversation
The old man,
Which is corrupt according to the deceitful lusts;
[rest]

And be renewed
In the spirit of your mind;
[hold]

And that ye put on the new man,
Which after God is created
In righteousness
And true holiness.
[2 bar instrumental – bass only]

[Bridge – vv.25–27]
Wherefore putting away lying,
Speak every man truth with his neighbour:
[call]
For we are members one of another.
[response – repeat softly]

Be ye angry, and sin not:
[hold]
Let not the sun go down upon your wrath:
[rest]
Neither give place to the devil.
[drop to drums + bass]

[Verse 4 – vv.28–30]
Let him that stole steal no more:
[rest]
But rather let him labour,
Working with his hands the thing which is good,
That he may have to give to him that needeth.
[hold]

Let no corrupt communication
Proceed out of your mouth,
But that which is good to the use of edifying,
That it may minister grace unto the hearers.
[rest]

And grieve not the Holy Spirit of God,
Whereby ye are sealed
Unto the day of redemption.
[2 bar instrumental swell]

[Final Chorus – vv.31–32]
Let all bitterness,
And wrath,
And anger,
And clamour,
And evil speaking,
Be put away from you,
With all malice:
[hold]

And be ye kind one to another,
Tenderhearted,
Forgiving one another,
[build]

Even as God
For Christ's sake
Hath forgiven you.
[hold — fade out on repetition]
```
