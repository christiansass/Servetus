# Two Years — Film Concept (Rapture Time-Travel Script)

## Summary

Christian Sass describes a film concept he has been carrying for years — a speculative video work that opens in 2028, depicts a person watching the rapture happen via live stream, and then sends that person back two years (to now, 2026) to find Christian making his ministry videos. The film is designed as both evangelism and a fulfillment-of-prophecy demonstration.

The concept is anchored to a 2008 short film called *Epic 2014*, which Christian intends to update and use as the film's opening sequence.

## The Logline

A person close to believing — but not quite there — watches the rapture happen alone, through a screen, in 2028. Social media explodes. The Word of God is outlawed online within hours. God gives this person a second chance: they are sent back two years to now, where they encounter Christian Sass making these videos.

> "This person came close to believing and is asking God for a second chance. Doesn't want to go through the Tribulation, so God sends them back to now meeting me making these videos."

## Why Social Media Is Central

> "Imagine what it would be like to be home alone during the return and watching it blow up on social media. You understand the whole world would see. Does it make sense? That's a fulfillment of prophecy, right there."

> "I want you to just hypothesize what social media would do in that moment — how fast the Word of God would get outlawed online."

The "every eye shall see" prophecy is fulfilled not in a physical gathering but through global real-time media — a post-COVID shut-in alone at a screen.

## The Opening Sequence — Epic 2014

The film opens with an updated version of *Epic 2014*, a speculative 2008 short film about the future of media (set in the year 2014). Christian's plan:

- Replace the original opening line ("It is the best of times... in the year 2014") with "in the year 2028"
- Rewrite the script to incorporate everything *Epic 2014* missed: the iPhone, OSINT, fifth-generation warfare, the digital shadow, AI
- Post the video as if it came from 2028 — as a video "from the future"

> "Part of the virality of this is that I'm going to post it as a video from the future, from 2028. So what happens if I'm right and that video's on the internet?"

> "Rewriting this script as the opener for my film has been something God put on my heart years and years and years ago, more than 10 years ago. I'm just now seeing how it all fits."

The film would cover the history of the internet from 1989 (World Wide Web) through to 2028, speculating on where things land — Trump, Elon, AI, autonomy, the image of the beast.

## The Image of the Beast Frame

The conversation around this concept leads into a theological exchange about AI and robotics as potential fulfillment of the "image of the beast" (Revelation 13):

> **Christian:** "The Antichrist will set up an idol, except that idol will be mechanical and robotic."
> **Ken:** "And it can speak."
> **Christian:** "And it will be worshiped. People are already worshiping it in their phone."

Ken's framing: "AI is Satan's attempt to counterfeit God's ability to create free will in human beings."

Christian's response: "I don't plan to carry Goliath's sword home as a trophy. I'm gonna pound it into a plowshare."

## The Platform: Word in Film

The updated Epic 2014 and the film itself are intended for publication on "Word in Film" — the video platform Christian is building to host ministry-related content. Every video he would ever recommend to Ken is already hosted on this server.

## The Stewardship Frame

The film, like the entire Servetus/research project, is understood as stewardship of specific gifts for a specific window:

> "Me doing this is me stewarding the talents that he gave me."

The knowledge base, the videos, and the film are designed to outlast the rapture — to serve as a resource center for those left behind, and to be available again upon return for the millennium.
