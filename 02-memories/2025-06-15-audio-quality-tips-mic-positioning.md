# Audio Quality Tips — Mic Positioning and DSLR Limitations

**Source:** 2025-06-15-Audio_Quality_Tips.md
**Date:** 2025-06-15

## Summary

Brief rewrite session — user provided a rough spoken note about audio issues and asked for a concise, helpful version.

## Key Points

- **Proximity to recording device matters** — physical distance affects audio quality significantly
- **Isolate camera from audio input** — don't rely on camera-mounted mics for quality capture
- **DSLR top-mounted mics** are problematic — breathing directly down into them causes audible breath sounds
- **External mic** is the correct solution for DSLR setups, or be extremely conscious of breathing
- **Desk recording:** primarily an issue of audio gear arrangement when seated

## Original Context (Rough)

"When I shoot on my DSLR, I breathe directly down into the top-mounted microphones — I can't trust those mics."
