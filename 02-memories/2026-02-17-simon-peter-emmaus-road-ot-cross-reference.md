# Simon Peter Cross-Reference — Emmaus Road OT Usage (1–2 Peter)

**Source:** 2026-02-17-Messianic_Prophecies_in_Luke.md
**Date:** 2026-02-17

## Summary

The user asked for a cross-reference of every OT passage Simon Peter cites in his epistles (1–2 Peter) mapped against what Messiah could have expounded on the Emmaus road — based on the theory that Simon Peter was the unnamed disciple traveling with Cleopas (Luke 24). The theory has Patristic support: Origen (Contra Celsum 2.62) describes the scene as "Simon and Cleopas," making Simon a plausible candidate, though Luke does not name the second disciple. The mapping reveals that Peter's OT usage clusters tightly around the same "Moses → Prophets → Psalms" arc Jesus describes in Luke 24:44.

## Key Points — Simon as Emmaus Disciple

- Luke names Cleopas but not the second disciple
- Origen identifies the pair as "Simon and Cleopas" — earliest Patristic witness to this identification
- Luke 24:34 reports a separate appearance to Simon (Peter) before the two Emmaus disciples return — this complicates but does not eliminate the theory
- "Simon" in tradition could be Simon Peter or another Simon/Simeon

## Peter's OT Usage Mapped to Emmaus Arc

**Torah — Atonement / sacrificial logic:**
- Ex 12:5 (Passover lamb without blemish) → 1 Pet 1:19 ("precious blood… as a lamb without blemish")
- Ex 24:8 (blood of the covenant) → 1 Pet 1:2 ("sprinkling of the blood")
- Deut 21:22–23 (hanged on a tree is accursed) → 1 Pet 2:24 ("bare our sins in his own body on the tree")
- Lev "Be holy" → 1 Pet 1:16 (direct quotation)
- Ex 19:5–6 / Deut 7:6 (royal priesthood, peculiar people) → 1 Pet 2:9

**Writings — Rejection → Vindication:**
- Ps 118:22 (rejected stone→cornerstone) → 1 Pet 2:7
- Isa 28:16 (Zion foundation stone) → 1 Pet 2:6
- Isa 8:12–14 (stone of stumbling) → 1 Pet 2:8 and 3:14–15

**Isaiah 53 — Densest "why crucified" text:**
- 1 Pet 2:22–25 saturated with Isa 53 wording: innocence, sin-bearing, stripes/healing, sheep astray

**Hosea — Covenant people from no-people:**
- Hos 1:9–10 / 2:23 → 1 Pet 2:10 ("not a people… had not obtained mercy")

**Eschatology — new creation/glory:**
- Isa 65:17 / 66:22 (new heavens and new earth) → 2 Pet 3:13

**Peter's use of Noah, Sodom, Balaam (2 Peter):** Grounds eschatological warning in Moses — consistent with Emmaus-style "Scripture must be fulfilled" logic

## Four Lanes Peter's OT Clusters Into

1. **Atonement / sacrificial logic from Torah** — Ex 12, Ex 24, Deut 21
2. **Suffering righteous one → vindication** — Isa 53, Ps 118, Isa 28, Isa 8
3. **Covenant-people identity** — Ex 19, Deut 7, Hosea
4. **New creation / final restoration** — Isa 65–66 via 2 Peter 3

This grid maps directly onto Luke 24's summary: necessary suffering → Scripture fulfilled → forgiveness preached to all nations → glory/restoration.
