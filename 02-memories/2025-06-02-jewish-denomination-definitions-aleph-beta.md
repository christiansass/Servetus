## Denomination Definitions

| Label | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Modern Orthodox** | Traditional belief/practice; engages modern world and secular society |
| **Baal Teshuva** | "Master of return" — formerly secular Jews who returned to Orthodox observance |
| **Yeshivish** | Ultra-Orthodox identifying with yeshiva/Torah-learning culture; more insular |
| **Liberal Orthodox** | Orthodox halacha, but more progressive views on social issues/gender |
| **Conservative** | Conserves Jewish tradition with some modernization; varies widely in practice |
| **Reform** | Liberal; emphasizes ethics over ritual; personal autonomy; adapts to modern life |
| **Reconstructionist** | Progressive; Judaism as evolving civilization; community decision-making focus |
| **Other** | Unaffiliated or independent |
| **Not Jewish** | For non-Jewish users exploring the site |
