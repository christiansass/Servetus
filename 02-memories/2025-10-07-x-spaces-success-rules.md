# X Spaces — Rules for Running a Successful Space

**Source:** 2025-10-07-XSpaces_success_rules.md
**Date:** 2025-10-07

## Summary

Comprehensive guide to running effective X (Twitter) Spaces for brand building, community engagement, or outreach.

## Key Rules (Summary)

1. **Define purpose and theme** — know why you're hosting; one-sentence "Space mission statement"
2. **Prepare and promote in advance** — schedule 2–3 days early; post teasers; tag speakers
3. **Start on time and set expectations** — tell listeners the format at the top
4. **Manage audio quality** — quiet room, good mic; mute guests when not speaking
5. **Keep energy up** — acknowledge new arrivals; ask listeners questions; don't let silence drag
6. **End with a clear CTA** — tell people what to do next (follow, DM, join community, etc.)
7. **Record and repurpose** — X records Spaces; use the audio for clips, transcripts, and content
