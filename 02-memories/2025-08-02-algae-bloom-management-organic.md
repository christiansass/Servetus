# Algae Bloom Management — Organic / Non-Chemical Methods

**Source:** 2025-08-02-Algae_bloom_management.md
**Date:** 2025-08-02

## Summary

Photo-based query about managing algae blooms in a water feature (public/natural pond context) without chemicals. Comprehensive organic strategy provided.

## Natural Management Strategies

### 1. Shade and Surface Cover
- Floating plants: duckweed, water lilies, water hyacinths
- Compete with algae for nutrients; reduce light penetration
- Artificial shade balls or netting as fallback

### 2. Aeration and Water Circulation
- Solar-powered aerators or wind-powered surface fountains
- Bottom diffusers and bubblers stir water, reduce nutrient buildup
- Avoid over-aeration that disturbs sediment

### 3. Biological Control (Algae-Eating Species)
- **Grass carp** — filamentous and planktonic algae
- **Tilapia** — effective in warmer climates
- **Koi** — moderate algae grazing
- **Snails** — graze on algae; monitor populations
- Note: Check local regulations before introducing species

### 4. Additional Methods (in source file)
- Barley straw application
- Nutrient runoff reduction (phosphorus/nitrogen from lawn, runoff)
- Manual removal for thick mats
