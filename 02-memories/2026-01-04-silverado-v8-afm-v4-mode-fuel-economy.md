# 2008 Silverado V8 — AFM / V4 Mode Fuel Economy

**Source:** 2026-01-04-AFM_and_V4_Mode.md
**Date:** 2026-01-04

## Summary

Explainer on GM's Active Fuel Management (AFM) system for the 2008 Silverado V8. No manual control exists — V4 mode is entirely automatic. Session covers how AFM decides to engage and what the driver can do to maximize V4 time.

## How AFM Works

- **V8 mode** → all cylinders (power/load)
- **V4 mode** → 4 cylinders deactivated (fuel savings)
- Transitions are fully automatic; no driver override from the factory

## AFM Engages V4 When ALL of These Are True

- Light throttle
- Steady cruising (flat ground)
- Warm engine
- No heavy electrical/mechanical load
- Typically 40–70 mph, gentle acceleration

## Tips to Maximize V4 Time (No Mods)

1. **Throttle discipline** — slow, smooth acceleration; avoid micro-corrections
2. **Use cruise control** — holds AFM engagement better than a human foot on flat highway
3. **Speed window** — V4 active ~45–65 mph; drops out above ~70 or below ~40
4. **Reduce load** — remove weight, don't tow, turn off A/C, maintain tire pressure
