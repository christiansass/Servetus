# Generational Wealth — Lifetime Gifting vs. Estate Transfer

**Source:** 2025-11-20-Generational_wealth_distribution.md
**Date:** 2025-11-20

## Summary

Explainer on the financial and stewardship rationale for giving to children while alive (annual tax-free gifts) vs. holding wealth until death (subject to estate taxes).

## Core Principle

> Wealth that is stewarded intentionally and transferred *during* your lifetime has far more power to build generational strength than money hoarded until death.

Both secular financial planning and biblical stewardship converge on this view.

## Four Arguments for Lifetime Gifting

1. **Tax efficiency**: Annual gift tax exclusion (U.S.) allows tax-free transfers; dying with a large estate triggers estate taxes that can claim a significant portion
2. **Impact**: You can see the effect and guide how the money is used while you're alive to mentor
3. **Training**: Children learn wealth stewardship by actually handling it, not by inheriting it without context
4. **Compounding**: Money given early has more years to compound for the next generation

## Caveats

- Country-specific; always requires qualified tax professional for implementation
- Giving must be measured and intentional — not indiscriminate wealth transfer
