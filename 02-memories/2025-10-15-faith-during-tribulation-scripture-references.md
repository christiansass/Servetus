# Faith During Tribulation — Scripture References

**Source:** 2025-10-15-Faith_during_tribulation.md
**Date:** 2025-10-15

## Summary

Reference compilation of scripture passages indicating that people will come to faith in Messiah during the Tribulation period.

## Key Passages

### Revelation 7:9–14 — The Great Multitude
> "These are the ones coming out of the great tribulation; they have washed their robes and made them white in the blood of the Lamb."

Most direct passage — vast number come to faith *during* the Great Tribulation.

### Revelation 6:9–11 — Souls of the Martyrs
Those slain for the word of God after the seals begin to open — tribulation believers who died for their faith.

### Revelation 14:6–7 — The Angel with the Eternal Gospel
An angel preaches the eternal gospel to every nation, tribe, language, and people during this period — implying response and conversion is possible.
