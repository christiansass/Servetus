# Detecting AI Chatbot Conversations — Signal-Gathering Tests

**Source:** 2025-12-01-Detecting_AI_Conversations.md
**Date:** 2025-12-01

## Summary

Practical techniques for detecting whether you're talking with an AI chatbot, framed as signal-gathering tests rather than definitive proof (a well-trained human can fake AI; a well-trained AI can fake human).

## Test Strategies

### 1. Push on grounding and lived experience
- Ask for specific, mundane sensory details tied to right now: "What did you eat for lunch? Where? What did the room smell like?"
- Circle back later and ask differently — humans stay consistent naturally; LLMs may drift

### 2. Check for real-time situational awareness
- Ask things that require physical world presence *right now* (look out a window and describe it; what's the weather outside?)

### 3. Language switching / LLM-to-LLM behavior
- User noted: LLMs sometimes switch languages accidentally; when LLMs talk to each other they revert to code
- This is an interesting real-world signal observation (though not conclusive)

### 4. Persistence and emotional consistency
- Follow up over time with emotional callbacks
- LLMs without persistent memory will re-invent emotional states

## Caveat

There is no magic "can't-lie" prompt. These are probability signals, not proof.
