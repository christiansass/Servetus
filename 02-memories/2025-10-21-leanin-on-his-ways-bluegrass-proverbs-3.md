## Summary

Full bluegrass gospel song written contrasting "the ends justify the means" worldview with trusting God's plan. Scripture anchor: Proverbs 3:5–6. Written with intentional emotional arc and empathetic pacing.

## Song Title

**"Leanin' on His Ways"**

## Theme

God's plan is always better than ours. Stop controlling outcomes; join Him in His plan; look for His provision and blessing.

## Emotional Arc

1. **Conflict/Striving** — listener in weariness, trying to control outcomes
2. **Awakening** — recognizing the cost of self-reliance
3. **Surrender** — releasing control; "leaning not on own understanding"
4. **Joy/Assurance** — landing on Proverbs 3:5–6; confident rest in God's direction
