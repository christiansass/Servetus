# Mural Calendar Templates — Shared Workflow with Ken

## Summary

The session opened with Christian Sass screen-sharing a Mural calendar board. Ken Daniels was seeing it for the first time and immediately recognized the value — comparing it to finding prior art on a problem he had been solving manually for years.

## The Opening Exchange

The session begins mid-action, with Christian navigating a Mural calendar board. Ken discovers he can copy and paste the template cards to his own personal boards:

> **Ken:** "Okay, can I copy and paste these things on my personal board?"
> **Christian:** "Oh, totally, man, I've given you a template to work with."

Ken's reaction to seeing the scope of what Christian had already built:

> "You've done so much work that I was like, doing myself, that I'm like, finding this, and I'm like, shit. I don't need to do all this work. It's like somebody who wants to know every usage of the word Pneuma in the New Testament. He did all the work already, right? Don't gotta reinvent the wheel."

## The Calendar Problem

Christian is navigating a full-year Mural calendar and discovers an error — the board ran to the 31st of February (a nonexistent date), causing all subsequent dates to be off by a day. He is correcting it live during the session:

> "Oh, it went to the 31st — that's why that wasn't even February. I'm totally missing a month."

This manual correction process becomes a direct illustration of the problem Servetus is designed to solve: doing by hand what software should do automatically.

> "So doing this manually is a pain in the butt, which is why I'm writing software to do this automatically. I am writing software that creates these things called story maps. I call this whole thing a story map."

## Templates as Shared Infrastructure

Christian makes and shares Mural templates as collaborative infrastructure — not just for his own use but as reusable tools others in the ministry community can adopt. Ken's recognition of this is significant: he had been building similar structures from scratch, spending significant time on what turns out to be pre-solved work.

The Mural calendar/template workflow represents the manual, pre-Servetus version of what story map generation is meant to automate.

## Connection to the Broader Vision

The Mural board is described as "bespoke, handcrafted" — the current state. Servetus is the machine that replaces the hand-crafting with automatic generation from atomized voice input. See [[2026-02-28-servetus-voice-to-storymap-loop]] for the full architecture.
