## Summary

Very brief session: a photo of a loop pedal was submitted for identification. GPT confirmed it as a Boss RC-505 Loop Station (tabletop form factor). The distinguishing features cited were the large color screen upper left, five vertical faders (one per loop track), green-lit transport buttons, and desktop format. Likely the original RC-505 based on silver/gray coloring, though the RC-505mkII is possible.
