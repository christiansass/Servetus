# Antique Sewing Machine Identification — Domestic Improved High Arm

**Source:** 2025-05-15-Antique_Sewing_Machine_ID.md
**Date:** 2025-05-15

## Summary

Photo-based identification of an antique sewing machine labeled "Improved High Arm."

## Identification

**Most likely:** Domestic Sewing Machine Company — "Domestic Improved High Arm" model
**Period:** Late 1800s to early 1900s

## Key Identifiers

- Label: "Improved High Arm" — common late 19th century term for improved arm height
- Gold scrollwork / Victorian floral decals — characteristic of Domestic, White, and Singer competitor machines
- Cast iron body (typical of this era)
- Possibly installed in a treadle cabinet

## Manufacturer

**Domestic Sewing Machine Company** — Norwalk, Ohio; leading American sewing machine maker in the late 1800s; known for durability and smooth operation.

## Next Steps for Positive ID

1. Find serial number (metal plate near base or under arm)
2. Check manufacturer name/emblem on machine bed or treadle base
3. Inspect cabinet for brass plaques or additional decals
