# Truth Social = Mastodon Fork — Idea to Integrate into Nextcloud Ecosystem

**Source:** 2025-10-04-Truth_Social_software_details.md
**Date:** 2025-10-04

## Summary

Two-part session: (1) confirming Truth Social's technical underpinning, and (2) developing a vision for forking these open-source platforms and integrating them into a self-sovereign Nextcloud-based internet ecosystem.

## Truth Social Tech Stack

- **Base**: Modified/forked version of Mastodon (open-source federated social networking)
- **Frontend**: Soapbox (instead of Mastodon's native UI)
- **License**: Mastodon is AGPLv3 — Truth Social legally obligated to provide source access
- **Modifications**: Some Mastodon features stripped (polls, visibility options)

## The Vision (Binary Ranch Direction)

> "Have your own social media, have your own YouTube, have your own website, have everything you use the internet for under one roof — and it's all open source."

Key components to fork and integrate into Nextcloud:
- **Mastodon/Truth Social fork** → federated social media
- **PeerTube** → alternate YouTube
- **Nextcloud** → file storage + collaboration
- **Soapbox frontend** → custom UI layer

Strategy: Fork each component *for the purpose of integration*, not just standalone deployment. Build a unified sovereign internet under one roof.

## Observation

This vision predates / parallels what became Binary Ranch Cloud's positioning around privacy-focused, sovereignty-aligned cloud services.
