# Binary Ranch and Servetus as Sovereignty Infrastructure: Building a Wheelchair for My Mind

## Summary

This segment of the conversation began as a discussion of robotics and human interface devices — Christian trying to identify the correct computer science term for mice, keyboards, and other devices humans use to interact with machines. The conversation moved through Grok and then Claude live as AI interlocutors. The correct term landed on HCI (Human-Computer Interaction) rather than HID.

From that technical thread, a much larger self-understanding emerged: Christian described himself as someone who has done computer science thinking his entire life but has been blocked from expressing it through the standard interface (keyboard / typed text). His ADD makes typing a cognitive disruptor — it "unplugs his brain." Voice, by contrast, allows him to hyper-process. He noted that the Cintiq graphics tablet has always been his preferred HID as an artist — direct, spatial, no translation layer.

He framed Servetus and Binary Ranch in this context: Servetus is the proper interface for how his brain actually works. He is building it as a "wheelchair" for himself first — a memory and knowledge system his brain literally does not maintain on its own — and then as a replicable template for others.

The critical counterpoint he raised: while he has been building toward responsible AI infrastructure (Servetus, Binary Ranch), the broader public is rushing headlong into ungoverned AI dependency — handing over their data, their context, their lives — with no guardrails, no data ownership, no retrieval when things go wrong. He sees this as the core Binary Ranch argument: the infrastructure for sovereign, responsible AI interaction doesn't exist yet, so he is building it.

A new development was also mentioned: Jim found a full-stack developer at Foundation Church who is now part of the Binary Ranch build. Christian described him as "rocket fuel." He also noted that Vern Edwards (of The Way International) called him, they spoke for half an hour, and his next step is to invite Vern onto Binary Ranch (the platform) for ongoing calls.
