# M1 MacBook Pro — Upgradeability (Cannot Upgrade)

**Source:** 2025-12-19-M1_MacBook_Pro_upgradeability.md
**Date:** 2025-12-19

## Summary

Quick reference: M1 MacBook Pro cannot be upgraded after purchase — RAM and SSD are soldered to the chip.

## Key Facts

- **RAM**: Unified memory, part of the M1 SoC — soldered, cannot be replaced or added
- **SSD**: Soldered on most M1 MacBook Pros — cannot be swapped
- **Processor**: Fixed at time of manufacture — no upgrades possible
- **Specialty**: Third-party microsoldering services exist but are risky, expensive, and void warranty

## Practical Alternatives

- External fast SSDs via Thunderbolt/USB-C
- Configure with higher specs at purchase — this is the only reliable upgrade path
