# Apologetics Course and Church History Map — Miro Board Overview

The core of this session was a walkthrough of Christian Sass's Miro-based church history and apologetics research chart — described as 30 years of accumulated work spanning fellowship notes, timeline research, and documentary sources. This was the first time Nick Bates had been granted access to the main research board.

## The Biblical Research Center (Miro Board)

Christian maintains two primary Miro boards:
1. **"First Century Fellowship"** — the public-facing resource; every home fellowship teaching organized on a timeline from November 2022 onward. Shared with Ken and Nick as editors during this session.
2. **The deeper apologetics/church-history board** — previously restricted to one other person; Nick now given access. Contains 15–20+ teachings and a visual church history timeline from Martin Luther to the present.

The board is described as simultaneously:
- A record of every fellowship teaching Christian has given
- A breadcrumb trail for an apologetics class
- The framework for an entirely new modular class

> "This entire wall that starts here is nothing more than a bread crumb trail of every fellowship I've taught those kids. Here's every teaching in a visual form and all the resources." — Christian Sass, 21:08:35

## Church History Timeline — Key Movements Covered

Christian walked the group through his color-coded church history timeline. Key movements and their visual coding:

| Color | Movement |
|-------|---------|
| Blue | Christian's own lifetime / personal journey |
| Yellow | Hebrew Roots movement (~1950s to present) |
| Red | Azusa Street / Charismatic / Pentecostal line |
| Purple | First Century Fellowship |

**Movement landmarks noted:**
- Azusa Street Revival — origin of charismatic / Pentecostal movement; unknown to Nick
- Vesper Chimes radio program → became The Way International in 1955
- Association of Vineyard Churches — began 1977
- New Apostolic Reformation (NAR) — coined 1996; contemporaneous with Victor Paul Wierwille's era but opposing theological trajectory
- Lonnie Frisbee — introduced Eastern mysticism into post-charismatic church; John Wimber facilitated it
- Bethel Church (Redding, CA) — Trinitarian; criticized for counterfeit spirituality; video clip played during session

**Michael Rood** identified as "damn near a founder of the Hebrew Roots movement" — came out of The Way International, as did Christian.

**Michael Servetus** — introduced during discussion of John Calvin:
> "He published THE ERRORS OF THE TRINITY and was burned at the stake by John Calvin." — Christian Sass, 20:54:56

## The Council of Nicea and the Calendar

A sustained section covered how the Council of Nicea (~325 AD, Athanasius era) institutionalized Trinitarian doctrine and simultaneously suppressed the Hebrew calendar:

> "The devil spiked a touchdown when he basically instituted Nicea and took the Jews and kicked them out and denatured the calendar and changed all of the Feast Days of God into pagan festivals overnight." — Christian Sass, 20:45:46

The Visigoths, Ostrogoths, and Vandals are identified as the last Aryan (non-Trinitarian, biblical Unitarian) Christian communities — hunted and "Christianized" (forced into Trinitarian belief) as Rome consolidated power.

## Apologetics as the Teaching Foundation

Christian's primary commitment to Ken and Nick is to give them the equivalent of a self-guided seminary education in apologetics — enough to hold a conversation with any seminary graduate:

> "I want you guys to know everything I know, and then decide if you still want to do seminary. But I think I can give you a shortcut, and I can make it so that you can hold a conversation with somebody who went to seminary, which is an equipping that is apologetics. That's my responsibility to you men, okay, as founders of whatever this is." — Christian Sass, 21:27:05

Christian himself would attend Wheaton College for a theology doctorate but cannot sign a Trinitarian statement of faith as required.
