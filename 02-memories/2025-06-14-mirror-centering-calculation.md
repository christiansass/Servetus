**Source:** 2025-06-14-Mirror_Centering_Calculation.md

## Summary

Partial audio session (two audio stubs at start) followed by text calculation for centering a mirror on a wall above a bar.

## Calculation 1: Wall Centerline

**Wall width:** 11′ 9½″

- Convert: 11 × 12 = 132″ + 9.5″ = **141.5 inches**
- Divide by 2: **70.75 inches = 70¾″**

→ **Measure 70¾″ from either end to snap the centerline.**

## Calculation 2: Mirror Vertical Centering (Above Bar)

- **Height of wall above bar:** 3′ 7″ = 43 inches
- **Height of mirror:** 26¾″

Steps:
1. Available space: 43″
2. Space above and below mirror: 43 − 26.75 = 16.25″ → 16.25 ÷ 2 = **8⅛″** margin top and bottom
3. **Bottom of mirror sits at 8⅛″ above the bar surface**
