# Prayer as Business Partnership with God

**Source:** 2025-06-07-Prayer_as_Business_Partnership.md
**Date:** 2025-06-07

## Summary

Christian asked ChatGPT to summarize his analogy about prayer for his friend Nate — framing prayer not as a request list but as a divine business partnership model.

## The Analogy (Summary for Nate)

### 1. Prayer as Transcendent Communion
Not a transactional request list. Prayer is **being with God** — like stepping into His office for holy alignment, not just outcomes.

### 2. Prayer as Operating Standard for Leaders
Equivalent to a company's core operating process. **No decisions, meetings, or partnerships should proceed without this communion.** Leadership without prayer is disqualified.

### 3. God as Ultimate Landlord and CEO
Vivid illustration: Imagine the owner of the Merchandise Mart offering you the top floor, rent-free, with a direct line to call him. That's God's offer.
> "Move in. I own the universe. Come ask Me. I'll guide you."

### 4. Shifting from Self-Driven to Spirit-Led
Key personal pivot: moving from **presenting plans to God for His blessing** → **waiting on God for the plans themselves**.
- Applied to Seven Talents: shaped more by God's will than by Christian's own agenda.

### 5. Legacy for Sons
"My children will one day say: God did that for my dad." Credit goes to the visible hand of God — a legacy of trust, dependency, and divine orchestration.
