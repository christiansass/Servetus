## Summary

Research into the theological sources behind Victor Paul Wierwille's Unitarian/non-Trinitarian beliefs and the historical roots of the Oneness Pentecostal movement.

## Wierwille's Theological Sources

Wierwille **synthesized** existing anti-Trinitarian ideas rather than originating them. Key influences:

1. **E.W. Bullinger** — Dispensational, ultradispensational framework; biblical precision emphasis; Wierwille extended his scriptural literalism into anti-Trinitarian conclusions
2. **George Lamsa** — Aramaic Bible translator; non-Trinitarian leanings in Eastern Christian tradition; Wierwille used his work for alternative readings of Christ's identity
3. **Apostolic / Oneness Pentecostal streams** — Wierwille diverged doctrinally but shares rejection of Nicene Trinity
4. **Internal codification** — "Power for Abundant Living" class; self-reinforcing scriptural interpretation; rejected Nicene and Athanasian Creeds
