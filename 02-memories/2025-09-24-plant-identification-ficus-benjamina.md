## Primary Plant: Ficus Benjamina (Weeping Fig)

**Identifying Features:**
- Glossy, oval, pointed leaves with smooth edge
- Long slender branches, arching, many fine twigs
- Naturally bushy; may thin indoors if stressed

**Why It Looks Sparse:**
- Leaf drop triggered by: movement, temperature changes, inconsistent watering
- Needs bright indirect light; low light causes leaf loss
- Low indoor humidity causes leaf loss in winter

**Care Tips:**
1. Bright spot near window; avoid harsh direct sun
2. Even moist soil; let top inch dry between waterings
3. Mist occasionally or use humidifier
4. Prune bare branches to encourage new growth
