# Property Maintenance Week — Task List and Development Planning

**Source:** 2025-06-24-Maintenance_and_Development_Tasks.md
**Date:** 2025-06-24

## Summary

Planning session to generate a full task list for a dedicated property maintenance week, plus a separate future development list.

## Maintenance Week Tasks

### Outdoor / Driveways / Gravel
- Order and spread additional truckload of gravel
- Grade existing gravel
- Spray or burn weeds along gravel paths and driveways

### Fencing
- Inspect and repair all fences (posts, segments)
- Weed and spray under fence lines

### Orchard & Garden
- Mow fully to base of orchard tree trunks
- Remove 4–5 dead/nonproductive orchard trees
- Demarcate and prepare garden area
- Protect surviving asparagus and BlackBerry patches

### Landscaping & Beds
- Weed all beds around house and barn
- Refresh landscaping rock (where cloth is exposed)
- Remove dead rock roses; consider new plantings

### Barns & Outbuildings
- Inspect and repair Morton building: holes, doors, flashing, critter control
- Clean and reorganize tools; remove raccoon waste
- Evaluate smaller barn for wood repairs and structural reinforcement
- Investigate and address water issues at far barn
- Clean ditch area (old appliances, dead animals, debris)

### Garage & Entry Areas
- Remove all contents, power wash, disinfect interior
- Repaint garage interior
- Repair garage door, back door, and railing
- Fix/replace back entrance light
- Relocate hay bales to proper storage
