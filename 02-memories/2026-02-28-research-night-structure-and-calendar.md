# Research Night — Structure, Scheduling, and Nextcloud Calendar

The final segment of the session (21:31–21:44) was dedicated to committing the group's research sessions to a formal schedule. What had been spontaneous hangouts and catch-up calls would become structured, recurring "research nights."

## Decision Made

**Day:** Monday nights
**Participants:** Christian Sass, Ken Daniels, Nick Bates — and wives included
**Cadence:** Weekly to start; aspiration of 2–3 times per week
**Duration:** Two hours per session
**Name:** "Research Night" (explicitly not called fellowship)

> "Let's agree to a time that we do a research night together and make it part of our weekly schedule." — Christian Sass, 21:31:56

> "How about you and I say we are married — we're going to include the wives, and we'll do this on Monday nights." — Christian Sass, 21:33:42

> "I think we should do it multiple times a week, personally." — Ken Daniels, 21:41:37

## What "Research Night" Is

Christian described it as an intermediate step in the production pipeline:

> "What it is is it's old fellowship notes being shaped towards becoming a class. I think it's the step in the middle." — Christian Sass, 21:42:12

Ken Daniels reframed it in the affirmative:

> "For all intents and purposes as of right now, this very moment we are in a class." — Ken Daniels, 21:42:29

The content for the research nights is 30 years of Christian's biblical research stored in Miro. The goal is to walk through each teaching module, re-teach it to Ken and Nick so they fully understand and can teach it themselves, then produce a final recorded version.

## Administrative Note — Robert's Rules

Separate from research nights, Christian proposed a monthly or bi-weekly administrative meeting with Roberts Rules structure to handle First Century Fellowship organizational matters:

> "We should probably do, like an administrative Roberts Rules, not a fellowship, but we need to do that probably every other week, if not monthly." — Christian Sass, 21:34:05

## Nextcloud Calendar Setup (Live During Session)

Christian created a shared Nextcloud calendar titled "First Century" during the call and shared it with Nick Bates and Ken Daniels (and Allison Sass).

Key observations from the setup:
- Nextcloud calendar now has a "New Task List" feature (noted as new since last session).
- Calendar can sync to phones; Christian frames it as a "full replacement" for Google Calendar.
- First event created: Monday research night, labeled "Research Night — First Century, first meeting where we're going to start doing our weekly biblical research nights."

> "Instead of getting together with debates to just do catch up, we're going to actually start going over the past 30 years of research that I have in Miro." — Christian Sass, 21:40:00

## Agenda Planning

Christian committed to preparing an agenda for the next 4–5 sessions in advance:

The modular structure of the Miro board makes this tractable — each node on the board can become a session.
