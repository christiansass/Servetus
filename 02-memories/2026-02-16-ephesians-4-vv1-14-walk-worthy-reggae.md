# Walk Worthy — Reggae Arrangement of Ephesians 4:1–14

**Source:** 2026-02-16-Ephesians_4_Reggae_Song.md
**Date:** 2026-02-16

## Notes

Scripture-faithful KJV reggae arrangement. One-drop feel, ~72–76 BPM, E minor or D minor. Lines allowed to breathe. Stage directions in brackets.

## Lyrics

```
[Intro – Spoken, light drums + bass]
I therefore, the prisoner of the Lord,
Beseech you,
That ye walk worthy
Of the vocation
Wherewith ye are called.
[rest 1 bar]

[Verse 1 – vv.1–3]
With all lowliness and meekness,
[hold]
With longsuffering,
Forbearing one another in love;
[rest]

Endeavouring to keep
The unity of the Spirit
In the bond of peace.
[2 bar instrumental]

[Chorus – vv.4–6]
There is one body,
And one Spirit,
[hold]
Even as ye are called
In one hope of your calling;

One Lord,
One faith,
One baptism,
[rest]

One God and Father of all,
Who is above all,
And through all,
And in you all.
[hold – let band swell]

[Verse 2 – vv.7–8]
But unto every one of us
Is given grace
According to the measure
Of the gift of Christ.
[rest]

Wherefore He saith,
When He ascended up on high,
He led captivity captive,
And gave gifts unto men.
[2 bar instrumental]

[Chorus – softer first half, build]
There is one body,
And one Spirit,
Even as ye are called
In one hope of your calling;

One Lord,
One faith,
One baptism,
One God and Father of all.
[hold]

[Verse 3 – vv.9–10]
(Now that He ascended,
What is it
But that He also descended first
Into the lower parts of the earth?)
[drop instruments slightly]

He that descended
Is the same also
That ascended up far above all heavens,
That He might fill all things.
[rest 2 bars]

[Verse 4 – vv.11–12]
And He gave some, apostles;
And some, prophets;
And some, evangelists;
And some, pastors and teachers;
[hold between lines]

For the perfecting of the saints,
For the work of the ministry,
For the edifying
Of the body of Christ:
[2 bar instrumental – bass lead]

[Bridge – vv.13–14]
Till we all come
In the unity of the faith,
And of the knowledge
Of the Son of God,
[build]

Unto a perfect man,
Unto the measure
Of the stature
Of the fulness of Christ:
[hold]

That we henceforth be no more children,
Tossed to and fro,
And carried about with every wind of doctrine,
[rest]

By the sleight of men,
And cunning craftiness,
Whereby they lie in wait to deceive.
[drop to drums + bass]

[Final Chorus – full band]
There is one body,
And one Spirit,
Even as ye are called
In one hope of your calling;

One Lord,
One faith,
One baptism,

One God and Father of all,
Who is above all,
And through all,
And in you all.
[repeat softly – fade]
```
