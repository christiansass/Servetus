## Summary

Campaign strategy session for a GoFundMe (or GiveSendGo) supporting a friend whose 94-year-old Korean War veteran father had a misdiagnosed brain bleed requiring emergency neurosurgery, with collapsed insurance coverage. Christian helping as campaign advisor.

## Campaign Core

- **Subject**: 94-year-old Korean War–era veteran
- **Crisis**: Misdiagnosed brain bleed → emergency neurosurgery + insurance coverage collapse
- **Caregiver**: Daughter carrying full financial, medical, and logistical responsibility
- **Financial runway**: 2–3 months before collapse
- **Outcome**: Real chance at meaningful recovery and quality of life

## Emotional Core (GPT Framing)

> This campaign is about *buying time* — time for healing, time for VA benefits, time for dignity.

Not tragedy — **faithful endurance under impossible pressure**.

## Campaign Strengths Identified

- Veteran story (broad appeal)
- Real, documented crisis (not vague hardship)
- Specific, urgent financial need
- Daughter's personal dedication as through-line narrative
