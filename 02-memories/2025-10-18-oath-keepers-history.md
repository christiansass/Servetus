**Source:** 2025-10-18-Oath_Keepers_history.md

## Summary

Balanced, detailed history of the Oath Keepers organization — origins, ideology, structure, controversies, and legal outcomes.

## Founding

- Founded 2009 by Elmer Stewart Rhodes (Stewart Rhodes)
- Rhodes: Yale Law graduate, former U.S. Army paratrooper
- Name references the oath military/law enforcement take to "defend the Constitution against all enemies, foreign and domestic"
- Incorporated in Nevada as nonprofit (disputed tax-exempt status)

## Ideology

- Frame themselves as constitutional defenders resisting "tyranny" from federal overreach
- Often hold conspiracy-oriented views about federal government intentions
- "Oath over orders" principle — refuse unconstitutional commands
- Promote "constitutional sheriff" concept

## Membership

- Primarily current and former military, law enforcement, first responders
- Also general public with similar views

## Controversies and Legal Outcomes

- Active at various political flashpoints (Bundy Ranch standoff, Ferguson, etc.)
- January 6, 2021: Stewart Rhodes and others convicted of seditious conspiracy
- Rhodes sentenced to 18 years in prison (2023)
