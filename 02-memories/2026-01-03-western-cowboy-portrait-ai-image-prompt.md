## Prompt Spec (Consolidated)

### Wardrobe
- Weathered red felt cowboy hat (required)
- Denim or canvas work shirt
- Traditional Western vest (preferred) OR period-accurate Western duster coat
- Outer layer must contrast shirt and hat in color
- Aged but not dirty — iconic Western identity, not modern farmhand

### Facial Identity Preservation (Critical)
- First reference image (eyes visible, no sunglasses) = PRIMARY anchor
- No face replacement, no archetypal cowboy face, no beautification, no de-aging
- Preserve: jaw width, nose shape, ear position, eye spacing, neck length, wrinkles, pores, smile lines, beard texture
- Preserve genuine smile — keep tooth shape, asymmetry; do not neutralize to stoic look
- Skin: naturally textured, real — visible pores, uneven tone, micro-contrast

### Eyewear Rule
- Remove sunglasses; reveal eyes from primary reference
- Preserve corrective glasses if present in primary reference

### Background / Output
- Pure black background, no gradients
- Isolated for compositing
- Transparent PNG with clean alpha if supported

### Lighting
- Cinematic, directional, raw — closer to high-end photojournalism than studio beauty
- Preserve skin texture and micro-contrast; no diffusion or softening

### Color Grading
- Earthy, cinematic: warm browns, muted tans, natural reds, deep shadows
- Clear color separation between hat, shirt, and outerwear

### Mood
- Quiet authority, independence, resolve, frontier dignity

### Technical Quality
- Ultra-high resolution, photorealistic, editorial documentary realism
- Poster-ready framing (NOT poster-retouched)
