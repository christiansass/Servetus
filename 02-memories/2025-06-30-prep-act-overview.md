# PREP Act Overview — Public Readiness and Emergency Preparedness Act

**Source:** 2025-06-30-PREP_Act_Overview.md
**Date:** 2025-06-30

## Summary

Quick explainer on the PREP Act — what it is, who it covers, and how it was used during COVID-19.

## What Is the PREP Act?

**Full name:** Public Readiness and Emergency Preparedness Act
**Enacted:** 2005 (U.S. federal law)
**Authority:** Gives the HHS Secretary power to issue declarations providing liability immunity for medical countermeasures during public health emergencies

## Key Provisions

### 1. Liability Immunity
- Covers manufacturers, distributors, program planners, licensed healthcare providers
- Applies to: development, manufacture, testing, distribution, administration, and use of "covered countermeasures"
- Immunity holds **even if something goes wrong** — unless willful misconduct

### 2. Covered Countermeasures
- Vaccines, drugs, medical devices, diagnostics for treating/diagnosing/preventing emergency-related conditions
- Must be FDA-approved, EUA-authorized, or investigational

### 3. Emergency Declarations
- HHS Secretary issues specific declarations per threat (e.g., COVID-19, anthrax, smallpox)
- Declarations specify: countermeasures covered, time period, geographic area, protected parties

### 4. Compensation Program
- Injuries from covered countermeasures → **CICP (Countermeasures Injury Compensation Program)**
- Limited compensation; narrower than civil lawsuit remedies

## COVID-19 Application

- Central to U.S. pandemic response
- Covered: Pfizer, Moderna vaccines; therapeutics; masks; test kits
- Protected: pharmacies, hospitals, volunteers administering vaccines/treatments
