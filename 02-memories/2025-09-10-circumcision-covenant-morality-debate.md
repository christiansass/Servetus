# Circumcision — Covenant Sign vs. Morality Debate

**Source:** 2025-09-10-Circumcision_and_morality_debate.md
**Date:** 2025-09-10

## Summary

Theological discussion responding to a friend's objection that circumcision is immoral and thereby calls God's authority into question.

## Biblical Framework

- **Genesis 17:9–14**: Circumcision given as covenant sign to Abraham, not as universal moral law
- **Not universal**: Only commanded for Israel; not a command to all nations
- **Fulfilled in Christ**: New Covenant makes physical circumcision spiritually irrelevant (Galatians 5:6; Romans 2:28–29; Colossians 2:11)

## Addressing the "Immorality" Claim

- The pain/consent objection is understandable from modern individualistic ethics
- Biblically: God includes children in covenant acts without individual consent (also Passover, baptism in some traditions)
- God commanded it for redemptive purposes that transcend human reasoning
- Since it was explicitly commanded by God, calling it immoral challenges His authority, not affirms it

## Correct Framing

> "Circumcision was once required by God for Israel as a covenant sign, but in Christ it has been fulfilled and is no longer binding."

**Today's position**: Neither sin by circumcising nor by abstaining — it's no longer binding under the New Covenant.

## Key Scriptures

- Genesis 17:9–14; Acts 15; Galatians 2; 5:6; Romans 2:28–29; Colossians 2:11
