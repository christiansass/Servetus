# Federal Woodworks — Naming and Branding Strategy

**Source:** 2026-02-09-Company_name_help.md
**Date:** 2026-02-09

## Summary

Discovery and naming session for a woodworking business built as a legacy brand dedicated to the founder's son's memory. Target markets: residential heirloom buyers and institutional/GSA buyers (disabled veteran advantage). The session worked through naming directions, trademark research, and domain strategy, landing on **Federal Woodworks** as the primary brand.

## Key Points

- **Hidden signals from discovery**: legacy/memorial intent, American heritage aesthetic, institutional buyer profile, wood-burnable brand mark required — not "Etsy artisan" energy
- **Names evaluated**: Heritage Furniture (rejected — domain squatted at $20K, `.furniture` TLD also brokered), Foundry (rejected by user), Founders Furniture Co (trademark risk from Thomasville adjacency), Founders Woodworks (available, acquired defensively)
- **Final primary brand: Federal Woodworks** — domain `federalwoodworks.com` registered; Illinois state trademark search returned clear
- **Defensive domain acquired: `founderswoodworks.com`** — to be used as narrative/story layer, not competing brand; redirect to Federal Woodworks
- **Brand attributes**: quiet authority, permanence, restraint, heirloom quality, civic dignity — "the name on invoices, catalogs, and buildings"
- **Logo direction**: wood-burnable maker's mark, furniture silhouette (not tools), architectural geometry, strong serif typography
- **Miro brand prompt written**: "Develop the brand identity for Federal Woodworks — a modern American furniture company rooted in federal-era ideals of permanence, restraint, and craftsmanship…"
- **Illinois entity registration recommended**: Federal Woodworks, LLC
- **Next asset identified**: the maker's mark / burn stamp design brief

## Domain Stack

- `federalwoodworks.com` — primary (registered)
- `founderswoodworks.com` — defensive/narrative (registered)
- Recommended defensive: `federalwoodworks.co`, `federalwoodworks.us`
