# Western Cowboy Portrait — AI Image Prompts (Ken)

**Source:** 2026-02-13-Western_Cowboy_Portrait.md
**Date:** 2026-02-13

## Summary

The session produced detailed AI image generation prompts for a cinematic western cowboy portrait of a specific subject referred to as "Ken" (described as "the executive"). The workflow emphasized strict facial identity preservation using a primary reference image (eyes visible, no sunglasses) with secondary images only for confirmation — no face averaging or blending. Two prompt versions were generated: the base western portrait and an upgraded "sheriff-executive" variant adding a pocket watch, western bow tie, and full duster coat.

## Key Points

- **Subject:** Ken — styled as frontier lawman / executive archetype
- **Primary prompt rules:**
  - First reference image = facial identity anchor (eyes, smile, proportions)
  - Sunglasses in secondary images = ignore; never infer eyes from behind lenses
  - No beauty retouching, no symmetry correction, no de-aging
  - Preserve: jaw width, nose shape, ear position, wrinkles, pores, beard texture, smile asymmetry
- **Base wardrobe:** Weathered red felt cowboy hat, denim/canvas work shirt, western vest or duster — contrasting colors, no monochrome
- **Upgraded "sheriff-executive" wardrobe:** Full-length charcoal/deep brown duster, cloth/leather western bow tie, classic pocket watch with chain, red felt cowboy hat
- **Background:** Perfectly uniform pure black for compositing
- **Lighting:** Cinematic directional, documentary realism — no diffusion, no glam lighting, preserve skin micro-contrast
- **Color grading:** Earthy cinematic — warm browns, muted tans, natural reds
- **Output:** Vertical portrait, knees-up with generous headroom, 1024×1792
- **Negative prompt includes:** Skin smoothing, face replacement, sunglasses, dirty clothing, modern fashion, logos, background texture
