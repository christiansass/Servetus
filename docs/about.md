## Servetus

Servetus is an integrated large language model orchestrator designed to run alongside Nextcloud. It turns a self-hosted cloud into a fully sovereign, LLM-driven assistant -- one that understands your files, your calendar, your contacts, and the full context of your life operating system.

Servetus is not a chatbot. It is a structured processing engine with memory, behavioral rules, and a vault-based architecture that keeps every decision auditable and every record yours.

## Binary Ranch

Binary Ranch is the home lab and development environment where Servetus is built, tested, and run. It is a single-operator infrastructure: one person, one server, full sovereignty.
