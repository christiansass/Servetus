# Projects Spec

## Altitude
**Level 04** — Work clusters.

## Purpose
Projects are active work containers. They group related events, resources, and progress toward a goal.

## Characteristics
- **Goal-oriented**: Working toward something
- **Active or archived**: Has a lifecycle
- **Cross-referencing**: Links to events, arcs, and daily logs
- **Defined in config**: Project index lives in `config/projects.md`

## When to Use
- Ongoing work with deliverables
- Collaborations
- Initiatives with defined scope
- Anything you're actively "working on"

## Relationship to Arcs
- **Projects** = what you're doing (work)
- **Arcs** = the story of what's happening (narrative)

A project might span multiple arcs, or an arc might touch multiple projects.
