# Arcs Spec

## Altitude
**Level 05** — Narrative threads.

## Purpose
Arcs are the story layer. They thread together events into meaningful narratives — the "why" behind the "what."

## Characteristics
- **Narrative structure**: Beginning, middle, end (or ongoing)
- **Event-backed**: Composed of linked events
- **Meaning-making**: Adds interpretation and context
- **Cross-cutting**: Can span multiple projects

## When to Use
- A relationship evolving over time
- A career chapter
- A health journey
- A creative pursuit
- Any "story" you're living through

## Relationship to Projects
- **Projects** = what you're doing (work, tasks, deliverables)
- **Arcs** = the story of what's happening (narrative, meaning)

Example: "Word and Film history" is an arc. Individual productions within it are projects.
