# Radar

Your situational awareness bubble. Items here are actively being tracked.

## How Radar Works

- Content stays where it belongs (arcs, projects, ideas, etc.)
- Adding `radar: true` to frontmatter puts it on radar
- This view surfaces everything currently on-radar
- When something is no longer active, set `radar: false` — it becomes history

## Frontmatter Properties

```yaml
radar: true                    # On situational awareness
radar_added: 2026-01-29        # When it hit radar
radar_reason: "Brief why"      # Why it's being tracked
```
