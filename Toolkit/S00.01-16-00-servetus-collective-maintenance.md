# Servetus Collective Maintenance Protocol

## Purpose

Nextcloud Collectives are the shared operational record for Binary Ranch.
They are read by team members, partners, and security reviewers.
Servetus maintains them — but does not own them. Every write is approved
by a human before it happens.

---

## The Rule

**Servetus never writes to a Collective without explicit approval.**

Reads are always permitted. Suggestions are always permitted.
Writes require APPROVE: from an authorized person in the designated channel.

This applies even when the change is obviously correct. The approval is
the record that a human reviewed and authorized it.

---

## Approval Flow

1. Servetus identifies a needed change during session or collective review pass
2. Posts to IT group chat (or Christian 1:1 if no IT room): `APPROVE: [collective] / [page] — [reason]` with the proposed content inline or summarized
3. Human responds `APPROVE: [token]` or `REJECT: [token]`
4. On approval: Servetus writes the change and posts a confirmation with what changed
5. On rejection: Servetus logs the rejection in the session and moves on

---

## Notification on Write

Every time Servetus writes to a Collective it posts a notification:

```
📋 Collective updated: [Collective name] / [Page name]
What changed: [one-line summary]
Review: [link if possible]
```

Posted to: IT group chat + Christian 1:1
Never silently. Every write is announced.

## What Servetus Can Populate Autonomously (with approval)

- New partner/vendor profiles (from witness files)
- Infrastructure manifests (access, topology, security posture)
- Development standards (from vault specs and backlog)
- Session-generated documentation that has already been reviewed in chat

## What Servetus Never Populates

- Finance & Governance content (human-authored only)
- Legal or compliance records
- Customer-facing pages (requires product owner review)
- Goals and strategy (human intent, not derived)
