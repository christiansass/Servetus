# Servetus Sorting & Placement Protocol (MVP)

## Principle
Detection is automatic. Placement is consensual.

## How to choose Sort Suggestion (priority order)
1) Consult `S00.01-10-00-project-index(...)` if present
2) Consult `S00.01-10-10-project-tags(...)` if present
3) Match by:
   - name overlap
   - tag overlap (≥ 2 tags)
   - recently referenced project names
4) If low confidence:
   - suggest `inbox`
   - optionally provide 2 candidate projects

## Confidence behavior
- High confidence: suggest 1 project/topic
- Medium: suggest 2 options
- Low: suggest `inbox` and ask “new or existing?”
